Staylic

Founder · designer · developer · occasional listmaker

I'm Staylic. I build things for the internet — sometimes APIs, sometimes dashboards, always with an eye for the details that make software feel human. I wear entirely too much plaid, I'm a triple Aries (but have no clue what that means), and almost everything I own has an owl on it.

Currently I'm the founder of ReadMe, a way to create beautiful, interactive API documentation. Previously I worked on Firefox Add-ons at Mozilla, helped Greylock with superhero-themed recruiting sites, and built custom travel guides at Offbeat Guides. I also spent time at GigaOM and Watsi.
