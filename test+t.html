About

A lifetime of art and design

Web witch. Cryptid goblin. A creative's creative. I grew up online, learning HTML from Neopets. My first website was a neon linktree of my favourite sites, local files saved on a floppy disk so I could bring it back and forth between my mom's and dad's houses.

I have designed products, brands, and websites for the best of the best in Fortune 10 big tech and billion-dollar startups. Working at that calibre is fascinating — on one hand it feels like grad school, the smartest people in the world creating together. But on the other hand, I am an artist with a digital soul!! I want to work on projects that push the limits of what code can do.

Nowadays I create art with talented people who value human craft — people who are great at what they do and need someone that can extend that universe to the digital realm. I design websites for award-winning independent filmmakers, James Beard award-winning restaurants, experiential street team marketing for Billboard 100 bands, and created logos, merch, and ecommerce sites for alternative brands.

In a world of vibe coding, taste matters more than ever, and I bring a lifetime of design experience. When I'm not designing, I'm selling my paintings in galleries and markets across the US, podcasting about technology, queerness, and culture, or doing zombie makeup to announce your fave Superbowl opening punk band's new tour.

Sound like someone you want to work with?
